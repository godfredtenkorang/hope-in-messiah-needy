About Our Foundation

An Independent Voice
for the Most Vulnerable

Hope in Messiah Needy Foundation is an independent social service functioning as a non-profit charity. Our charitable activities were motivated by the concern of high rate of poverty in some part of our country and the need to provide assistance.

Our Vision

Vision

The vision of Hope in Messiah Needy Foundation is to provide medical support to the Needy, portable water to destitute areas, aid to the Needy society and help orphans and persons with physical disabilities.

Our Mission

Mission

The Hope in Messiah Needy Foundation mission is to provide medical support to those in need, as well as portable water to communities in need, assistance to the Needy in society and assistance to those with physical disabilities and orphanages and showing them how Jesus Christ loves Us.

Our Goals

Goals

  • Assisting those in need with medical care in depraved areas
  • Providing communities in need with portable water
  • Providing assistance to those in need in society concerning their education
  • Assist those with physical disabilities as well as orphans
